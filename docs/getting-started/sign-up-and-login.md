## What is Sign Up & Log In?

Waplify lets you create an account using your email or Google account. Once signed up, you can log in anytime to manage your WhatsApp campaigns, contacts, and more.

## How to use it

### Create a new account

1. Go to [app.waplify.io](https://app.waplify.io) and click **Sign Up**
![The sign-up page: name, phone number, email with Get OTP, password, and Sign up with Google](/img/docs/getting-started/sign-up-and-login-signup.png)
2. Enter your **name** and **phone number**
3. Enter your **email address** and click **Get OTP**, then type the 6-digit code we email you
4. Create a **password** and click **Sign Up**. You are logged in and ready to start the setup

### Sign up with Google

1. On the sign-up page, click **Sign up with Google**
2. Select your Google account
3. If this is your first time, you will be asked to confirm your **name** and add your **mobile number**, then click **Complete Signup**
![Complete your signup: confirm your name and add your mobile number](/img/docs/getting-started/sign-up-and-login-complete.png)

### Log in to your account

1. Go to [app.waplify.io](https://app.waplify.io) and click **Log In**
2. Enter your **email** and **password**, then click **Sign In**
3. Or click **Sign in with Google** to log in with your Google account

### Reset your password

1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter your email address and click **Send Reset Code**
3. Enter the verification code sent to your email
4. Set a new password and click **Reset Password**
![The Forgot Password page with the Send Reset Code button](/img/docs/getting-started/sign-up-and-login-forgot.png)

## Tips & best practices

- Use a strong password with at least 8 characters
- If you sign up with Google, you can still set a password later from your account settings
- If you manage multiple businesses, Waplify supports multiple organizations — you can switch between them from the sidebar
- Keep your email address up to date so you can always recover your account
- Once you're signed in, your next step is to [connect your WhatsApp number](./connect-whatsapp.md) so you can start messaging customers

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use the same email for multiple accounts?

No, each email address can only be linked to one Waplify account. However, you can be a member of multiple organizations with the same account.

### What if I don't receive the verification email?

Check your spam or junk folder first. If you still don't see it, try again after a few minutes. Make sure you entered the correct email address.

### Can I change my email address later?

You can update your profile information from the **Settings** page after logging in.

### Is my data secure?

Yes. Waplify uses encrypted connections and secure authentication to protect your account and data.

### Can I invite my team members?

Yes! Once your account is set up, you can invite team members from the **Team** section. They will receive an email invitation to join your organization. Once your team is ready, check out how to [send your first campaign](./your-first-campaign.md).
