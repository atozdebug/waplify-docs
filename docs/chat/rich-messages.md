## What is a Rich Message?

A **rich message** is a chat reply that does more than plain text. In one window you can format your text, add a photo, video or document, and add buttons your customer can tap. A live preview shows how it will look on your customer's phone before you send it.

It is a quick way to share a brochure with a caption, or ask a question your customer can answer with one tap (for example **Yes** / **No**).

## How to use it

### Open the rich message window

1. Go to **Chat** and open the conversation.
2. In the toolbar under the message box, click the **Rich message** button (it says **Rich message - format, media, buttons** when you point at it). A **New message** window opens.
![The Rich message button, first in the toolbar under the message box](/img/docs/chat/rich-messages-toolbar.png)

The same window also opens when you:

- attach or drop a photo, video or document into the chat, or
- pick a [quick response](./quick-responses.md) that has media or buttons.

### Write and format your message

1. Type your message in the box (**Write your message**, or **Add a caption** when you have added media).
2. Select some text and use the buttons above the box:
   - **Bold** (Ctrl+B)
   - **Italic** (Ctrl+I)
   - **Strikethrough**
   - **Monospace**
![The New message window with formatting buttons, Add media and a live preview](/img/docs/chat/rich-messages-window.png)

### Add a photo, video or document

1. Click **Add media** and choose an image, video or document.
2. To swap it, click **Replace**. To take it off, click **Remove media**.

Audio files are sent on their own, without text or buttons, so they don't open this window.

### Add buttons

1. Under **Buttons**, click **Add a button**.
2. Choose one kind:
   - **Custom reply** (under **Quick reply buttons**): your customer taps it and their choice comes back to you as a reply. You can add up to 3.
   - **Visit website** (under **Call to action buttons**): opens a web page you choose. You can add 1.
3. Type the **Button text** (up to 20 characters). For a website button, also enter the **Website URL**, starting with `https://`.
![Three Custom reply buttons in the Buttons section, shown in the preview](/img/docs/chat/rich-messages-buttons.png)

You can't mix the two kinds in one message: use reply buttons or a website button.

### Check and send

1. Look at the **Preview** to see how your customer will see it.
2. Click **Send**, or press **Ctrl+Enter**.

To send it later, click the **arrow** next to **Send** and pick a time. See [Schedule Messages](./schedule-messages.md).

## Limits

| | Up to |
|---|---|
| Plain text message | 4,096 characters |
| Message with media or buttons | 1,024 characters |
| Reply buttons | 3 per message |
| Website buttons | 1 per message |
| Button text | 20 characters |

The counter under the message box shows how many characters you have used. If you go over, Waplify asks you to keep the message under the limit.

## Tips & best practices

- **Ask with buttons.** "Would you like a callback?" with **Yes** and **No** buttons gets more answers than a question the customer has to type a reply to.
- **Keep button text short and clear,** like **Book now** or **Talk to us**. Each reply button needs a different label.
- **Use bold for what matters,** like a price or a date, not for whole paragraphs.
- **Save messages you send often as quick responses** with media or buttons, so you can send them again in two clicks. See [Quick Responses](./quick-responses.md).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I send buttons when the 24-hour window is closed?

No. A rich message is a normal chat reply, so it can only be sent within the 24-hour reply window. When the window is closed, send an approved template instead.

### Why is my message limited to 1,024 characters?

WhatsApp allows up to 1,024 characters on a message that has a photo, video, document or buttons. Plain text can be up to 4,096 characters.

### Can I add a phone number button?

Not in a chat reply. You can add **Custom reply** buttons or one **Visit website** button. For other buttons, use a [template](../templates/creating-templates.md).

### Can I add an emoji or bold text to a button?

No. Button text can't have emojis, variables or formatting.

### What does my customer see when they tap a reply button?

Their tap comes back to you as a reply in the chat, showing the button text they picked, so you know what they chose.
