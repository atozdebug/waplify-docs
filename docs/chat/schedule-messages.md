## What is Schedule Messages?

Sometimes the right time to send a message is not right now. With **Schedule Messages** you write your reply in a chat, pick a date and time, and Waplify sends it for you. You can schedule text, photos, videos, documents, audio, messages with buttons and approved templates.

Scheduled messages wait in a strip above the message box, so you and your team can see what is coming and change it before it goes out.

## How to use it

### Schedule a message

1. Go to **Chat** and open the conversation.
2. Type your message. You can also attach a photo, video or document, or open the [rich message window](./rich-messages.md) to add buttons.
3. Click the small **arrow** joined to the **Send** button (it says **Schedule message** when you point at it). The **Schedule send** menu opens.
![The arrow next to Send opens the Schedule send menu](/img/docs/chat/schedule-messages-menu.png)
4. Pick one of the quick times, for example:
   - **Today at 1:00 PM** (shown in the morning) or **Today at 6:00 PM** (shown from 11 AM to 4 PM)
   - **Tomorrow at 8:00 AM**
   - **Next Monday at 8:00 AM**
5. Or click **Pick a custom time**, choose a date under **Select a date** and a time under **Select a time**, then click **Schedule send**.
![The Pick a custom time window with a date and a time](/img/docs/chat/schedule-messages-custom-time.png)

You see **Message scheduled**, and the message appears in the scheduled strip above the message box.

:::info
Times are in your own time zone. The **Pick a custom time** window shows which one at the top, for example "India Standard Time".
:::

### Schedule a template

Templates can be scheduled at any time, even when the 24-hour reply window is closed.

1. In the chat, open the template window (**Send Template Message**).
2. Choose an approved template and fill in its variables.
3. Click **Schedule** (the button with the calendar icon, next to **Send Template**).
![The Send Template Message window with the Schedule button](/img/docs/chat/schedule-messages-template.png)
4. Pick a quick time or **Pick a custom time**.

You see **Template scheduled**.

### See what is scheduled

When a chat has messages waiting, a strip appears above the message box. It shows how many are scheduled (for example "2 scheduled") and when the next one goes out, with the start of its text.
![The strip above the message box showing 2 scheduled messages](/img/docs/chat/schedule-messages-strip.png)

Click the strip to see every scheduled message in that chat, with its time. If a teammate scheduled one, you see **by** and their name.

### Change or cancel a scheduled message

Click the **⋮** menu next to a scheduled message:

- **Send now** sends it straight away.
- **Edit** lets you change the text and the time of a text message (including one with buttons). Click **Save** when done.
- **Reschedule** changes the time of a photo, video, document, audio or template message. Click **Save** when done.
- **Cancel** removes it. There is no "Are you sure?" step, so it is cancelled at once.

### When a message could not be sent

If Waplify can't send a message at its time, it stays in the strip marked **Not sent**, with the time it was due and the reason in red. Common reasons:

- **The 24-hour reply window had closed by the scheduled time.** Send a template instead.
- **The workspace's plan is not active,** so nothing can be sent.
- **The person who scheduled it can no longer reply to chats,** for example because they left the team.

Nothing is sent again by itself. Send the message yourself if it is still needed, then click **Dismiss** in its **⋮** menu to clear it. Messages that were not sent stay visible for 7 days.

## Rules and limits

- **Up to 30 days ahead.** And at least a minute from now.
- **Up to 20 scheduled messages per chat.** Cancel one to add another.
- **Normal messages must go out within the 24-hour reply window.** If the window will be closed by your chosen time, Waplify asks you to pick an earlier time or schedule a template instead. Quick times that fall after the window closes are not shown.
- **Templates can be scheduled at any time**, because they don't need an open window.
- **It is sent in your name.** The message goes out as the person who scheduled it. Waplify checks again at send time that you can still reply in that chat.
- **What can't be scheduled:** voice notes, locations, catalogs and call buttons. Send these straight away.
- **Who can change what:** you can change your own scheduled messages. Team members who can see all chats can also change or cancel anyone's.
- Available on every plan.

## Tips & best practices

- **Reply when your customer is awake.** If a message comes in late at night, schedule your answer for **Tomorrow at 8:00 AM** so it lands at the top of their chats in the morning.
- **Mind the 24-hour window.** For a follow-up two or three days later, schedule a template, since a normal message can't go out once the window closes.
- **Check the strip before you send by hand,** so the customer doesn't get the same message twice.
- **Use Send now** when plans change and the message should go out early, instead of cancelling and retyping it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I schedule a message for more than 30 days ahead?

No. You can schedule up to 30 days ahead. For something further out, schedule it later, or use a [campaign](../campaigns/creating-campaigns.md).

### Why can't I pick a later time for my message?

The 24-hour reply window will close before that time, and WhatsApp doesn't allow normal messages after it closes. Pick an earlier time, or schedule an approved template instead.

### What happens if I cancel a scheduled message by mistake?

It is removed straight away and can't be brought back. Write the message again and schedule it.

### Will the customer know the message was scheduled?

No. They receive it like any other message from your business, at the time you picked.

### Can my teammates see the messages I schedule?

Yes. Anyone who can open the chat sees your scheduled messages in the strip, with your name next to them. Only you, or a teammate who can see all chats, can change or cancel them.
