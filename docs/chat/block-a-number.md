## What is Block?

Blocking a number stops that person from messaging your business on WhatsApp. Their messages no longer reach your inbox, your chatbots or your team, and nothing is sent to them from Waplify: no replies, campaigns, sequences or chatbot messages.

Use it for spam, abusive messages, or anyone who keeps messaging after you have asked them to stop. You can unblock a number at any time.

## How to use it

### Block someone from a chat

1. Open the chat in **Chat**.
2. Click the **⋮** menu at the top right of the chat.
3. Click **Block number**.
4. Check the message and click **Block**.

<!-- screenshot: chat header menu with Block number -->

The chat is resolved and marked **Blocked**. Any drip sequence or chatbot running for that person stops.

### Unblock someone

1. Open the chat. A blocked chat shows **This number is blocked** where you normally type.
2. Click **Unblock number**, then **Unblock**.

You can also unblock from **Settings > Blocked numbers**.

<!-- screenshot: blocked chat with the Unblock number button -->

### See everyone you have blocked

Go to **Settings > Blocked numbers**. You will see each blocked number, who blocked it and when, and a button to unblock it.

<!-- screenshot: Settings > Blocked numbers -->

### Block a number that has never messaged you

1. Go to **Settings > Blocked numbers**.
2. Click **Block a number**.
3. Type the number with its country code, for example `+91 98765 43210`, and click **Block**.

### Let agents block numbers

By default, owners, admins and managers can block numbers. To let agents block too, an owner or admin can switch on **Agents can block numbers** in **Settings > Blocked numbers**.

## Tips & best practices

- **Blocking happens straight away in Waplify.** WhatsApp only lets a business block someone who messaged it in the last 24 hours. If it has been longer, Waplify blocks them now and WhatsApp blocks them too the next time they message you. You don't need to do anything.
- **Messages sent while someone is blocked are gone.** If you unblock them later, those messages don't arrive. New messages work as normal.
- **Unblocking doesn't restart automations.** Sequences and chatbots that stopped when you blocked someone stay stopped.
- **The person isn't told.** On their phone, messages to you show a single grey tick.
- **Block covers all your WhatsApp numbers.** If you have more than one number in Waplify, the person is blocked on every one of them.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I report a number to WhatsApp from Waplify?**
No. WhatsApp doesn't let any app report a number. If you also use the WhatsApp Business app on the same number, you can open the chat on your phone and tap **Block and report**.

**Why does a chat say "Blocked" when I didn't block it in Waplify?**
When a message fails because WhatsApp says the number is blocked, for example because someone blocked it in the WhatsApp Business app on your phone, Waplify marks the chat as blocked so your team knows. You can unblock it from Waplify.

**What happens to a campaign that includes a blocked number?**
Nothing is sent to that person. In the campaign report they show as **You blocked this number**.

**I blocked the wrong person. What do I do?**
Unblock them from the chat or from **Settings > Blocked numbers**. They can message you again straight away, and you can reply as normal.

**Does blocking cost anything?**
No. Blocking is free on every plan.
