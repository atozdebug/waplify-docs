Waplify is now on your Android phone. Your whole team can answer customers, assign chats and keep up with new leads when you are away from the computer. [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.waplify.app).

<!-- truncate -->

## What you can do in the app

- **See every chat in one inbox**, with tabs for your chats, unassigned chats and all open chats.
- **Reply with text, photos, the camera, videos, documents and voice notes.**
- **Use your quick replies and approved templates.** The app shows when a chat's 24-hour window has closed, so you know when a template is needed.
- **Assign, resolve and reopen chats**, or hand a chat back to your chatbot.
- **Get an alert with sound** the moment a customer writes. Choose which chats alert you, and set quiet hours.
- **See the ticks** on every message you send: sent, delivered and read.
- **Pin chats, mark them as unread, or call a customer** in one tap.
- **Switch between companies** if you manage more than one business.
- **Dark mode**, for easy reading at night.

## How to get started

1. Install **Waplify** from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.waplify.app).
2. Log in with the same email and password you use on the website.
3. Allow notifications, so you hear when a customer writes.

## Good to know

- The app is included in **every plan**, at no extra cost.
- You need a Waplify account to use it. New here? Sign up on [waplify.io](https://waplify.io) first.
- Campaigns, flows and billing stay on the website. The app is for chatting with your customers.
- The app is for Android only for now.
