You can now **block a number** from the inbox. A blocked number can no longer reach your inbox, your chatbots or your team, and Waplify sends nothing to it: no replies, campaigns, sequences or chatbot messages.

<!-- truncate -->

## How to block someone

1. In **Chat**, open the chat and click the **⋮** menu at the top right.
2. Click **Block number**.
3. Check the message and click **Block**.

The chat is resolved and marked **Blocked**. Any drip sequence or chatbot running for that person stops.

## Good to know

- **You can unblock at any time.** Open the chat and click **Unblock number**.
- **See everyone you have blocked.** In **Chat**, open the filter next to the search box and tick **Blocked numbers only**.
- **Who can block:** Owners, admins and managers. Agents can't.
- Available on every plan.

Full details are on the [Block a Number](/docs/chat/block-a-number) page.
