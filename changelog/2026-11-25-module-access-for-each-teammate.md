Roles are a good start, but people's jobs are rarely a perfect fit. Now you can change what one teammate can open, module by module, without changing their role. Let one agent see campaigns, keep one manager out of chatbot flows, or give a partner a view-only account.

![The Module access window with an Off, View and Manage choice for each module](/img/changelog/2026-11-25-module-access-for-each-teammate-1.png)

<!-- truncate -->

## How to use it

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Click the **⋯** menu on the person's row, then **Module access**.
3. For each module, pick **Off**, **View** or **Manage**. Their role's level is selected to start with.
4. Click **Save**. Their open tabs update on their own.

You can also do this while inviting someone: tick **Customise access** in the invite window.

## Good to know

- Owners and admins can change module access for Admins, Managers and Agents.
- There are 12 modules, from Inbox and Contacts to Media library and Developer. The Inbox levels are **Off**, **Read only** and **Reply**.
- **Manage** covers everyday work. Deleting still depends on the role.
- Teammates with changed access show a **Custom access** badge on the team list.
- Changing someone's role resets their module access. **Reset to role defaults** undoes your changes at any time.

Full details are on the [Roles & Permissions](/docs/team/roles-and-permissions#module-access-change-what-one-person-can-open) page.
