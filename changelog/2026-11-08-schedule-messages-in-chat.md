You can now **schedule a message** from any chat. Write your reply, pick a time, and Waplify sends it for you. It works for text, photos, videos, documents and approved templates.

![The arrow next to Send opens Schedule send](/img/changelog/2026-11-08-schedule-messages-in-chat-1.png)

<!-- truncate -->

## How to schedule a message

1. Open a chat and type your message.
2. Click the small **arrow** next to **Send**.
3. Pick a quick time, like **Tomorrow at 8:00 AM** or **Next Monday at 8:00 AM**, or click **Pick a custom time** to choose your own date and time.

To schedule a template, open the template window and click **Schedule** next to **Send Template**.

## See and change what's scheduled

A strip above the message box shows how many messages are waiting and when the next one goes out. Click it to see them all. Each one has a **⋮** menu where you can **Send now**, **Edit** (or **Reschedule**) or **Cancel**.

## Good to know

- **You can schedule up to 30 days ahead**, and up to 20 messages per chat.
- **Normal messages must go out within the 24-hour reply window.** If the window will be closed by then, Waplify asks you to pick an earlier time or schedule a template instead. Templates can be scheduled at any time.
- **If a message can't be sent**, it shows **Not sent** with the reason, so you can send it again yourself.
- The message goes out in the name of the person who scheduled it.
- Available on every plan.

Full details are on the [Schedule Messages](/docs/chat/schedule-messages) page. New to the inbox? See [Sending Messages](/docs/chat/sending-messages).
