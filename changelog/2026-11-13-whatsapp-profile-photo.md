title: "Change Your WhatsApp Profile Photo From Waplify"

---

You can now change the **profile photo** of your WhatsApp business number right inside Waplify. It's the photo your customers see next to your name in WhatsApp.

<!-- truncate -->

## How to change it

1. Go to **Settings > WhatsApp**.
2. Click **Edit** on your number, then **Edit Business Profile**.
3. Under **Profile picture**, click **Upload photo** (or **Change photo**).
4. Click **Save Changes**.

## Good to know

- Use a **JPG, PNG or WEBP** image of at least 192 x 192 pixels, up to 10 MB. Waplify crops it to a square from the centre.
- Owners and admins can change it.
- If your number also runs on the **WhatsApp Business app** on a phone, change the photo in that app instead.

See [WhatsApp Account Info](/docs/account/whatsapp-account-info) for the rest of your business profile.
