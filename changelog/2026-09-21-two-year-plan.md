Every plan (Starter, Growth and Business) can now be paid for two years at a time, at **35% less per month** than quarterly. You pay once, your price stays locked for 24 months, and nothing is charged automatically.

<!-- truncate -->

## What it costs

| Plan | Per month | Paid once for 2 years |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | ₹649 | ₹15,576 |
| Growth | ₹1,169 | ₹28,056 |
| Business | ₹1,949 | ₹46,776 |

Prices are before 18% GST. International plans are $12, $25 and $45 a month.

## How to get it

On the checkout page, or in **Settings > Subscription**, use the switch above the plans and choose **2-Year**. The saving is shown on the switch, and it keeps the plan you had picked when you change the period.

## Good to know

- **Nothing renews automatically.** When your two years are nearly up, renew from **Settings > Subscription**. Renewing early adds the new period on top of the days you have left.
- **You can still upgrade.** Moving to a higher plan or to Lifetime part-way through gives you credit for the days you haven't used. Switching to a shorter period (Yearly or Quarterly) is available when your 2 years end.
- **Refunds:** direct purchases can be refunded in full within 15 days, the same as every plan.

Full details are on the [Subscription Plans](/docs/billing/subscription-plans) page.
