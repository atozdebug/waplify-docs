Need more people on your team than your plan includes? You can now buy **extra agents** without moving to a bigger plan. Each extra agent costs **₹499 a month** ($10 if you pay in US dollars), plus 18% GST when you pay in rupees.

<!-- truncate -->

## How the price works

- **You pay once.** Nothing is charged automatically.
- **You pay only for the time left on your plan.** For example, with 60 days left, one extra agent costs ₹984 plus GST.
- **Your extra agents end on the same day as your plan.** When you renew, they are added to your renewal price at ₹499 a month each:

| Plan | Price per extra agent at renewal |
|---|---|
| Quarterly (3 months) | ₹1,497 + GST |
| Yearly (12 months) | ₹5,988 + GST |
| 2-Year (24 months) | ₹11,976 + GST |

- **On a Lifetime plan**, your plan never ends, so extra agents are bought a year at a time: ₹5,988 per agent, plus GST. We remind you 7 days and 1 day before that year ends.

## How to add agents

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. In the **Plan limits** card, click **Manage agents**.
3. Choose how many extra agents you need.
4. Check the total and click **Pay**.
5. Invite your new team members from the **Team** page.

You can also open the same window from the **Team** page when your team is full. Only the workspace Owner can buy extra agents.

## Good to know

- Extra agents can't be removed or refunded part-way through. You can choose a lower number when you renew.

Full details are on the [Extra Agents](/docs/billing/extra-agents) page.
