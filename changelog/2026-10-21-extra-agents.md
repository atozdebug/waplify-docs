Need more people on your team than your plan includes? You can now buy **extra agents** for **₹499 a month each** ($10 in US dollars), plus 18% GST. You pay once, only for the time left on your plan.

<!-- truncate -->

## How it works

- **Pay once.** Nothing is charged automatically.
- **Pay only for the days left.** For example, with 60 days left on your plan, one agent costs ₹984 plus GST.
- **Renewing is simple.** When you renew your plan, your extra agents are included in the renewal price.
- **On a Lifetime plan**, you buy extra agents a year at a time: ₹5,988 per agent, plus GST.

## How to add agents

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. In the **Plan limits** card, click **Manage agents**.
3. Choose how many agents you need, check the bill, and click **Pay**.
4. Invite your new team members from the **Team** page.

Only the workspace Owner can buy extra agents.

Full details are on the [Extra Agents](/docs/billing/extra-agents) page.
