---
title: Contacts Can Join a Sequence on Their Own
tags: [sequences, automation, web]
---

You no longer have to pick contacts by hand every time. Set a simple rule once, like **Tag is `lead`**, and every contact who matches joins your sequence on their own and starts getting your messages.

<!-- truncate -->

## Why it helps

- **Set it once.** A welcome series keeps running for every new lead. Nothing to relaunch.
- **Nobody gets it twice.** A contact can join the same sequence only once.
- **Safe by default.** Contacts who opted out are never added.

## How to set it up

1. Go to **Sequences** and create a new sequence.
2. In the audience step, choose **Auto-enroll on event**.
3. Pick when a contact joins: **On create** (new contacts), **On update** (when a contact is changed), or **On both**.
4. Add a rule, for example **Tag is `lead`**.
5. Launch the sequence.

Now, when you tag someone `lead` from the inbox or a chatbot, they join the sequence straight away.

Full steps are on the [Creating a Sequence](/docs/sequences/creating-a-sequence#auto-enroll-on-event) page.
