title: Your Bot Steps Aside When Your Team Replies
tags: [inbox, chat, automation]

You can now make your chatbot go quiet as soon as someone from your team starts handling a chat. No more bot and person replying to the same customer at the same time.

<!-- truncate -->

## How it works

- The bot stops replying on a chat once it is **assigned to a teammate**, or a teammate **replies from the inbox**.
- Reply from the **WhatsApp Business app on your phone**? The bot can pause for 24 hours after each phone reply too.
- Your team sees a note in the chat that says why the bot is paused.

## How to turn it on

1. Go to **Settings > Inbox & routing**.
2. Switch on **Pause bot when a human replies**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

It is off until you turn it on, so nothing changes for you on its own. Only Owners and Admins can change it.

Full details are on the [Pause Bot When a Human Replies](/docs/chat/pause-bot-when-a-human-replies) page.
