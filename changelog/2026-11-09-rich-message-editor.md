Replying with more than plain text is now easier. The new **rich message** window lets you format your text, add a photo, video or document, and add buttons your customer can tap, all with a live preview before you send.

<!-- truncate -->

## How to use it

1. Open a chat and click the first button in the toolbar under the message box (**Rich message - format, media, buttons**).
2. Write your message. Use the buttons for **bold**, *italic*, ~~strikethrough~~ and `monospace`.
3. Click **Add media** to attach an image, video or document.
4. Click **Add a button** to add up to 3 **Custom reply** buttons, or one **Visit website** button.
5. Check the **Preview** and click **Send**. You can also schedule it with the arrow next to **Send**.

The same window opens when you attach a photo, video or document, or pick a quick response that has media or buttons.

## Also new

- **Template formatting shows in chat.** Bold, italic, strikethrough and monospace text in your templates now looks the same in your chats as it does on your customer's phone.
- **Reply buttons are easier to spot** in chat bubbles, with a small reply icon.
- **Website buttons on campaign messages open the right page.** If a campaign template has a personalised link, clicking its button in the chat now opens the exact link that customer received.

## Good to know

- A message with media or buttons can be up to 1,024 characters. Plain text can be up to 4,096.
- Buttons in a chat reply work only within the 24-hour reply window, like any other free-form message.
- Available on every plan.

Full details are on the [Rich Messages](/docs/chat/rich-messages) page. See [Sending Messages](/docs/chat/sending-messages) for more on replying to customers.
