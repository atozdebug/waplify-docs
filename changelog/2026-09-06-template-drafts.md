---
title: Save a Template as a Draft, and Never Lose One to a Disconnect Again
tags: [templates, web]
---

You can now park a template as a **draft** before it ever goes to Meta, come back to it later, and publish it when it is ready. And if a WhatsApp number is disconnected, its templates are no longer deleted: they are kept as drafts so you can put them back on a new number.

<!-- truncate -->

## Write it now, send it later

Building a template used to be all or nothing. You either submitted it to Meta for approval or you lost your work.

Now the template builder has **Save as draft** next to Submit for approval. A draft:

- never goes to Meta, so it cannot be rejected,
- keeps everything you put in it - body, header, footer, buttons, variables, carousel cards, uploaded media,
- can be edited as many times as you like.

Drafts live in their own **Drafts** tab in the template library, so they never clutter the templates you actually send with.

## Publishing a draft

Open a draft, press **Edit**, and it opens in the builder exactly as you left it. When you are happy, publish it onto a connected WhatsApp number and it goes to Meta for approval like any new template.

Carousel drafts work the same way.

A couple of things worth knowing:

- Publishing sends the template to Meta as a **new** template, so it goes through review again.
- If the name is already taken on the number you are publishing to, Waplify stops and tells you rather than letting Meta reject it.

## Your templates survive a disconnected number

This is the part that used to hurt.

When a WhatsApp number was disconnected - by you, by an admin, or by Meta severing it - **every template on that number was deleted**. The body copy, the buttons, the variable setup, the carousel cards and the media you uploaded all went with it, and there was no undo.

Now those templates are **kept as drafts** instead. They show up in the Drafts tab labelled with the number they used to live on, and you can publish them onto a new number whenever you are ready.

The same applies when a template disappears on Meta's side: it is drafted, not deleted.

And if you reconnect the same number later, Waplify recognises the drafts it rescued from it and quietly makes them live again.

## Good to know

- A draft carries no WhatsApp number until you publish it, which is why a draft and a live template are allowed to share a name.
- Drafts cannot be sent - not from a campaign, a sequence, the inbox, a flow, or the API. Every send path checks that a template is approved first.
- Everything else in the disconnect cleanup is unchanged. Only templates are preserved, because they are the one thing you cannot get back.
