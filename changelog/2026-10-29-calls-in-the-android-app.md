The Waplify Android app now takes **WhatsApp calls**. When a customer calls your business on WhatsApp, your phone rings like a normal call, even when the app is closed or the phone is locked. Update the app from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.waplify.app) to get it.

<!-- truncate -->

## What you can do in the app

- **Answer and make WhatsApp voice calls** with your customers.
- **Set yourself Available or Away.** It is the same status as on the website: change it in one place and the other follows.
- **See missed calls** and tap **Call back**.
- **Play call recordings**, on the plans that include them.

## How to switch it on

1. Update the Waplify app from Google Play.
2. In the app, go to **Settings > Notifications** and switch on **Ring this phone**.
3. Allow the **microphone**. On Android 14 and newer, also allow full-screen call alerts when the phone asks.

Calling must first be switched on for your WhatsApp number. Full steps are in [Take WhatsApp calls](/docs/calling/take-whatsapp-calls).
