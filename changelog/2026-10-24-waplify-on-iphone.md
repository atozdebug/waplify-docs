Using an iPhone? You can now add Waplify to your **Home Screen** and open it like an app, full screen, without typing the address each time. No App Store download is needed.

<!-- truncate -->

## How to add it

1. Open **app.waplify.io** in **Safari** on your iPhone and log in.
2. Tap the **Share** button. On newer iPhones, tap **•••** first.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

Waplify now has its own icon on your Home Screen. Waplify also shows these steps in a small card the first time you open it in Safari.

## Good to know

- This is the same Waplify you use in your browser, so everything you can do there works here too.
- On Android, use the [Waplify Android app](/changelog/2026/10/23/android-app) instead.
