The **Templates** page has a new look. Every template now shows as a card with a WhatsApp-style preview, so you see exactly what your customer will receive, and simple filters help you find the one you need.

<!-- truncate -->

## What's new

- **Preview cards.** Each card shows the message as it looks on WhatsApp, with its name, a green tick when approved, its category, language and date, and variables like `first_name` as green tags.
- **Filters at the top.** Pick a **Number**, a **Category** (Marketing, Utility, Authentication) and a **Status** (Approved, Pending, Rejected, Paused or Drafts). Each category shows how many templates it has.
- **All numbers at once.** The page opens on **All numbers**. If you have more than one WhatsApp number, each card shows which numbers can send it.
- **Sync from Meta.** Click the round arrows next to the search box to pull the latest status of your templates from Meta right away.
- **Create on Meta.** Click the small arrow next to **Create Template** to build a template on Meta's own website instead.
- **Drafts in one place.** Pick **Drafts** in the **Status** filter to see templates you saved but haven't submitted yet.

## Do I need to do anything?

No. Your templates are all still there. Open **Templates** to see the new page.

Full details are on the [Message Templates](/docs/templates/message-templates) page.
