You can now connect your **Shopify store** to Waplify. Remind shoppers who left items in their cart, send order updates on WhatsApp, collect leads from your store, and show your Shopify products in your WhatsApp catalog.

<!-- truncate -->

## What you can do

- **Abandoned cart reminders.** When a shopper leaves checkout without paying, Waplify sends them a WhatsApp reminder. You can see how many carts were abandoned, how many reminders went out, how many carts were recovered, and how much those sales were worth.
- **Order updates.** Send a WhatsApp message when an order is **confirmed**, **shipped** or **cancelled**.
- **Lead form.** Add a Waplify form to your store. When a visitor fills it in, they get your WhatsApp message straight away.
- **Your products on WhatsApp.** Sync your Shopify products into your WhatsApp catalog. When a customer sends you a cart on WhatsApp, Waplify can reply with a checkout link, with a discount code if you like.
- **Save shoppers as contacts.** Shoppers and buyers can be saved to your Contacts, so you can message them later.

## How to set it up

1. In Waplify, go to **Apps & Integrations** and open **Shopify**.
2. Install the Waplify app on your Shopify store and connect it.
3. Pick the WhatsApp number to send from.
4. Turn on what you need: **Lead Capture**, **Order Status** or **Cart Abandonment**, and choose an approved template for each message.
5. Send a test to your own number to check it.

Messages to shoppers use **approved WhatsApp templates**, because most shoppers haven't messaged you first. See [Message Templates](/docs/templates/message-templates).
