title: See Which Ad Brought Each Customer
tags: [inbox, contacts, automation]
---

When someone messages you by tapping your Facebook or Instagram ad, Waplify now remembers which ad it was. You can see it in the chat, use it in your chatbot, and send a follow-up to everyone who came from one ad.

<!-- truncate -->

## Where you'll see it

- **Inbox:** open the chat and look at **Came from ad** in the customer panel. Click **All ad taps** to see every ad they tapped.
- **Contacts and campaigns:** use the **Came from ad** filter to find everyone from one ad, then send them an offer.
- **Chatbot flows:** add the ad's headline to your messages, for example "Thanks for your interest in our Diwali Offer!"
- **Contact export:** your download now shows each contact's first and latest ad.

## Which plans include it

Growth and Business plans, Lifetime Gold, Platinum and Diamond, and plans bought before our current pricing. On other plans Waplify still saves the ad for every contact, so the history is ready the day you upgrade.

Full details are on the [More Ways to Start a Flow](/docs/flows-v2/more-ways-to-start#see-which-ad-brought-a-contact) page.
