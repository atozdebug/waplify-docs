Importing contacts from a spreadsheet now happens step by step. Waplify reads your file, lets you match its columns if needed, shows you what will be imported, and only then adds your contacts. You decide how to handle missing names, repeated numbers and opt-in status before anything is saved.

<!-- truncate -->

## How to import

1. Go to **Contacts** and click **Bulk Upload**.
2. Upload a **CSV or Excel** file. Need a starting point? Download the **Sample CSV** or **Sample Excel**.
3. **Match your columns.** If Waplify can't tell which column is the name or the phone number, it asks you. Pick **Don't import** for any column you don't need.
4. **Review.** Choose your options, then click **Import**.
5. When it's done, click **Download report** to see what happened to every row.

## Choices on the review step

- **Default country code** for numbers written without one.
- **Rows with no name:** skip them, use the phone number, number them (User #123456), or use one name for all.
- **Add tags** to everyone in this import.
- **Opt-in status:** use what's in your file, or mark everyone opted in or opted out.
- **Repeated numbers:** keep the first row or the last one.
- **Run automations for new contacts:** switch it off and nobody is messaged because of this import.
- **Download problem rows** to fix rows that can't be imported and try them again.

## Good to know

- Up to 2,000 contacts and 5 MB per file.
- Owners, admins and managers can import contacts. Agents can't.
- The same steps are used when you add contacts to a group from a file.

Full details are on the [Import Contacts](/docs/contacts/import-contacts) page.
