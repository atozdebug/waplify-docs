We fixed a set of problems in the inbox. Videos and voice notes now reach your customers, every message shows its ticks, and a slow internet connection no longer sends the same message twice.

<!-- truncate -->

## What's fixed

- **Videos send properly.** WhatsApp used to refuse some videos, especially ones recorded on a phone. They now go through.
- **Voice notes send properly.** Voice notes that WhatsApp sometimes refused are now delivered.
- **Ticks on every message.** Every message you send shows whether it was sent, delivered or read. A voice note that was played, or a link that was tapped, now counts as read.
- **No double messages.** If your internet is slow and you press send again, your customer still gets the message only once.
- **No missed messages.** When several customer messages arrive at the same moment, every one of them now shows in your inbox.

## Do I need to do anything?

No. These fixes are already live for everyone. Just refresh the page if Waplify was open in your browser.

New to the inbox? See [Sending Messages](/docs/chat/sending-messages).
